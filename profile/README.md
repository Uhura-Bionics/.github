## Core R&D Domains

We are solving the "Atypical Speech" problem by intergratinghigh-performance hardware and real-time working software.

###  Voice Synthesis & AI
We develop proprietary models to transform monotone mechanical signals into rich, personalized human voices.
* Real-time, low-latency neural vocoders and speaker-identity cloning to restore a user's original timbre.
* Mapping **physical inputs** (haptic, gesture, or residual muscle movement) to digital intonation, allowing for emotional expression.
* Optimizing NLP models for **real-time edge deployment** to assist in predictive speech for atypical speakers.

### Embedded Systems & DSP
Our hardware must be "invisible"—lightweight, long-lasting, and computationally powerful.
* **DSP pipelines** optimized for low latency to ensure natural conversational flow.
* Quantizing and deploying heavy neural models onto resource-constrained MCU/DSP environments.
